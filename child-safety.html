Child Safety Standards – Ahirwar Sambandh

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

Ahirwar Sambandh is a matrimonial application intended for adults. The app does not knowingly allow children under 18 to create or maintain matrimonial profiles.

1. Prevention of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

Ahirwar Sambandh has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content, profile, communication, or activity involving child sexual abuse material or exploitation is prohibited.

2. Reporting

Users can report profiles, content, or activity that may involve child safety concerns through the reporting features available in the app. Reports are reviewed and appropriate action may be taken, including removal of content, suspension, or termination of accounts.

3. Cooperation With Authorities

Where required by applicable law, Ahirwar Sambandh will cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement or child-safety authorities regarding suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation.

4. Age Requirement

Ahirwar Sambandh is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. Users must provide truthful information about their age and must not create a matrimonial profile for a person under 18.
